Milestones

ft_transcendence is an AI-driven platform for learning a foreign language. A short adaptive test places each learner at a CEFR level, and a roadmap then gives them lessons sized to their daily goal. This page summarises the milestones in progress: what each one delivers, how its issues depend on each other, and the data model behind it.

1 · Onboarding and Evaluation 2 · Course Roadmap and Lessons
Milestone 1

Onboarding and Evaluation

A new learner chooses a language and a daily goal of 10, 30 or 60 minutes, then takes an adaptive placement exam or sets their level themselves. The exam runs a binary search from B1 over 270 authored questions, with the server owning the clock. The learner then lands on the course home, and can switch between courses or add another language.

Milestone 2

Course Roadmap and Lessons

The course page: a daily goal page and an all lessons page over a seeded syllabus of A1 to C2 lessons per language, with results, the streak and level completion. The lesson page itself is the next milestone.
