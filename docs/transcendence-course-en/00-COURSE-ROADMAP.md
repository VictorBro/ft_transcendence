# 00 · Course roadmap

The times are realistic for someone who already knows a little about the web but is discovering
this code. "Reading" = reading the module. "Review" = opening the cited files,
doing the exercise and the self-assessment, then answering the oral questions out loud.

## The path in 10 steps

| Step | Goal | Modules | Reading | Review |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Understand the project as a whole | [01](01-PROJECT-INTRODUCTION.md) | 20 min | 20 min |
| 2 | Understand the stack | [02](02-TECH-STACK.md) | 35 min | 30 min |
| 3 | Understand the architecture | [03](03-GLOBAL-ARCHITECTURE.md), [04](04-CODEBASE-STRUCTURE.md) | 50 min | 45 min |
| 4 | Understand the frontend | [05](05-FRONTEND.md) | 45 min | 60 min |
| 5 | Understand the backend | [06](06-BACKEND.md), [08](08-AUTHENTICATION-AND-SECURITY.md) | 75 min | 90 min |
| 6 | Understand the database | [07](07-DATABASE.md) | 35 min | 40 min |
| 7 | Understand the features | [09](09-BUSINESS-FEATURES.md), [10](10-REALTIME-AND-COMMUNICATION.md) | 80 min | 90 min |
| 8 | Understand the complete flows | [12](12-END-TO-END-FLOWS.md) | 40 min | 45 min |
| 9 | Understand the infrastructure | [11](11-INFRASTRUCTURE-AND-DEPLOYMENT.md), [13](13-TESTING-AND-QUALITY.md) | 50 min | 45 min |
| 10 | Defend the technical choices | [14](14-TECHNICAL-DECISIONS.md), [15](15-ORAL-EXAM-PREPARATION.md) | 60 min | 120 min |

**Total: about 8 h of reading and 10 h of review**, i.e. 4 to 5 half-days.
Add 2 to 3 mock exams of 30 min ([16](16-MOCK-EXAM.md)).

## How to know you have finished a step

You have finished a step when you can, **without looking**:

1. draw its diagram on the board;
2. name the 3 most important files and say what they do;
3. answer the module's self-assessment questions;
4. explain what happens when a user performs the main action of that module.
