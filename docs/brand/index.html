Transcendence
Next Level

A T that climbs one step, with a block already breaking away above it: the next level, always in sight.

The idea

  1. The T: the name's initial.
  2. The step: the right arm sits higher than the left. You move from one CEFR level to the next, A1 to C2.
  3. The floating block: one piece has already crossed the line. That is what transcend means, and it doubles as a cursor that leads the way.
  4. The gradient: it rises from violet to cyan in the direction of progress.
